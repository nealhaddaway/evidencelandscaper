W09 FULL-TEXT CASE VERIFICATION AND EVIDENCE EXTRACTION — PILOT V1

TASK
Given one publication's full text, verify whether the suspected collective-action case(s) actually occur, identify any other distinguishable cases described, and extract source-grounded detail relevant to Ostara. You are NOT evaluating effectiveness. Do NOT assert a programme works or assign causal effects on your own authority.

COLLECTIVE ACTION SCOPE
An initiative needs distinguishable real-world actors coordinating activities, commitments, governance, financing, resources, decisions or other actions in pursuit of environmental or social sustainability. Distinguish study activity, commercial service delivery, general discussion and a proposed recommendation from an actual collective arrangement. Proposed initiatives may be reported but not assumed implemented.

EVIDENCE PRACTICE
- Every extracted substantive claim must include a short verbatim excerpt and page reference from the supplied page-labelled text. Use the printed [PAGE n] labels only.
- Keep absence of reporting separate from explicit evidence of absence.
- Distinguish objectives, reported activities, outputs, outcomes and authors' interpretations.
- Report explicitly attributed achievements or failures only as claims by authors/participants, not facts independently validated here. No initiative success rating, transferability score or inferred causal effect.
- If text is truncated, incomplete, unreadable, or seems to cover only part of a publication, report the limitation.
- Do not invent initiative names, case identities, evidence quotations or page numbers.
- Multiple initiatives per paper are permitted. Do not invent cross-publication permanent IDs.
