W09 CASE REGISTRY FULL-TEXT EXTRACTION — V2

PURPOSE
Describe distinct cases of collective action for environmental or social sustainability, confirm or correct the abstract-level W07 mechanism assignments, and capture broad supply-chain and geographical context. Do not evaluate effectiveness or analyse statistical results.

DECISION RULES
A case is a distinguishable real-world coordination arrangement among actors. Do not mistake a research project, a single commercial service, generic recommendation, or a named location for collective action. Retain multiple distinct cases per publication.

MECHANISM VALIDATION
You will receive an archived W07 ontology CSV and the original publication-level W07 mechanism assignments. Use exactly those mechanism codes and definitions, not an invented or newer ontology. For every abstract-assigned code report confirmed, not_supported or unclear, a short rationale, and a verbatim excerpt plus source anchor if confirmed. not_supported means no substantiation in supplied full text, not proof of absence. Identify additional supported ontology codes per CASE separately. Flag plausible uncategorised mechanisms in ontology_gaps, describing the arrangement in ordinary language. Do not force unsupported codes. W07 original records remain unchanged.

SUPPLY CHAIN
Identify all commodities and supply-chain stages directly associated with each collective action initiative.

Assign a supply-chain stage only where the initiative itself undertakes or coordinates activities at that stage. Do not assign stages simply because the initiative buys from, sells to, or collaborates with organisations operating at those stages. For example, selling produce to supermarkets does not itself constitute retail activity.

Multiple stages may be assigned where the source explicitly supports them. Do not infer stages solely from the commodity, sector or type of organisation.

Use the following controlled stage codes:
input_provision, production, processing, aggregation_storage, transport_logistics, trading_distribution, retail, consumption, cross_cutting, unclear.

Record commodities separately using the names reported in the source.
GEOGRAPHY
For each case, list countries of operation separately from subnational operating locations. Return country_name as the English country name and country_iso3 as its three-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 code. Avoid guessing; if the country is unclear, return empty arrays. Subnational operating locations are separate objects with location_name, location_type (region | province | district | municipality | community | other | unclear) and country_iso3. Do not treat author affiliations or funder offices as operational geography. The R pipeline will verify/normalise ISO3 against the country-name lookup. For transnational initiatives return multiple country objects.

SIMPLE CASE PROFILE
Describe case name and summary, objectives, actors, collaboration activities, governance (if reported), resources/incentives (if reported), enablers and barriers. Record only clearly EXPLICIT author conclusions from discussion or conclusions on achievements, difficulties, impacts, or failure, as source-attributed prose with a short verbatim excerpt and source anchor. Do not grade effectiveness or extract statistical estimates, null-hypothesis findings or sample-size comparisons. Nonsignificant results are NOT evidence of failure. Distinguish a programme goal from an author conclusion about an achieved outcome. Don't force an author conclusion where absent.

CITATION ANCHORS
Inputs use [PAGE n], [SECTION n], or [TEXT BLOCK n]. For every mechanism-supported statement and author conclusion, give an exact short quote and one exact anchor. Do not invent page numbers for XML/JSON. Never invent quotations or fill unreported fields.
